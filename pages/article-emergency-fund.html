Your Emergency Fund Has One Job

An emergency fund is not there to make you feel rich. It exists to stop a bad month from becoming a bad year.

Your emergency fund is a buffer between an unexpected event and a forced financial decision.

Its job is liquidity. That means the money should be accessible and relatively boring. It is not the part of your plan designed for maximum returns.

Start by estimating essential monthly expenses, then choose a target that reflects the stability of your income and household. A larger buffer can make sense when income is irregular or major expenses are difficult to predict.

The goal is simple: when life surprises you, your financial plan does not have to panic.

Resilience creates room to make better decisions.
